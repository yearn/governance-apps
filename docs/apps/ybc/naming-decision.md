# YBC Naming Decision

## Recommendation

Use:

- app name / slug: `ybc`
- route key: `/ybc`
- beta host: `ybc-beta.dao-ops.com`
- production host: `ybc.yearn.fi` (gated until feed-backed reads, launch writes, fork smoke, and production approval)
- display label: `Yearn Builder's Collective`

## Why

Unlike `teams`, YBC is already a strong branded proper noun inside the protocol surface.
It should stay short in URLs and explicit in product copy.
